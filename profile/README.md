# Sonix

> Teaching machines to listen.

We're a two-person engineering team in Norway turning the noise of the real
world into signal — from the microphone in your hand to the model in the cloud.

---

### What we're building

Sound carries more than we hear. Sonix is the system that captures it at the
edge, moves it reliably, and learns from it at scale — a full pipeline from
**sensor → storage → model**, built end to end by two engineers who like hard
problems and small teams.

That's all we'll say for now. The interesting part isn't public yet.
